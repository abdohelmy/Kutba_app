## Information we handle

**Readers:** You do not need an account to read khutbas. The app saves your selected mosque, reading position, text-size preference, and previously opened sermons on your device for convenience and offline reading. Saved sermons include glossary explanations and source notes.

**Mosque administrators:** We store your username, display name, password hash, and association with your mosque. We also store the mosque's name, city, and country, uploaded PDF or DOCX files, extracted text, translations, glossary entries, review notes, and publication records. Passwords are stored as hashes, not readable passwords.

**Technical information:** Our server and hosting provider may record IP addresses, request times, requested addresses, and error information when you connect. Requests can identify the mosque or sermon being accessed. Because Khutba concerns religious content, this information may reveal religious interests.

The app does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location. It does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

## How we use information

We use information to provide sermons, manage administrator accounts, translate and check uploaded content, support human review, remember reader preferences, and maintain and troubleshoot the service. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

## Service providers and public content

We use Google Cloud to host the backend and OpenAI to translate and check sermon text. Extracted Arabic text, proposed translations, relevant glossary entries, and source excerpts may be sent to OpenAI. The original uploaded PDF or DOCX is stored on our server; the translation workflow sends extracted text rather than that file to the model. Relevant passages may also be submitted through OpenAI's web-search service to find hadith references.

The service retrieves references from sources such as Quran.com and Sunnah.com. Opening an external source link connects you to that website, whose privacy policy applies.

Published translations, mosque details, glossary explanations, and source notes are available to readers, who may save or download them. The complete Arabic sermon and internal review notes are not shown in the public reader. Administrators should avoid uploading unnecessary personal or confidential information.

## Storage and deletion

Administrator accounts and associated records remain stored until removed following an administrator request or another applicable retention decision. Administrators can remove sermons from the active service; hiding a sermon does not delete it.

Technical logs are retained for **[LOG RETENTION PERIOD]**. Deleted information may remain in protected backups for **[BACKUP RETENTION PERIOD]**.

You can remove local app data using your device's storage controls or by deleting the app. Separately downloaded PDFs must be deleted separately. Saved offline sermons cannot be withdrawn from a disconnected device immediately; the app removes its cached copy after a successful refresh confirms that the sermon is no longer available. We cannot directly remove copies readers have exported or shared.
